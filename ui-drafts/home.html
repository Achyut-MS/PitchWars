About

Startup Showdown is Round 2 of Future Makers. Five roles, one company, one big idea. Work as a team, decide fast and keep your coins safe.

How to play

  • Briefing (3 min): meet your team. You may change roles here.
  • Ideation (3 min): everyone votes. The CEO makes the final pick.
  • Shop (7 min): the CFO decides each request. Wallet starts at 1,000 coins.
  • Competition (16 min): your products earn coins. Protect your company.
  • Graphs: there is no score. Compare how both companies played.

Role guide

  • CEO: picks the idea and chooses attacks.
  • CTO: builds the product features.
  • CFO: approves what the team buys.
  • CMO: builds the brand and ads.
  • COO: fixes risks and buys the shield.
